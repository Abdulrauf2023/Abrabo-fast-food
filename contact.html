Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details for guests, UBIDS students, drivers, and event organizers.

Is parking available for cars and motorcycles?

expand_more
Yes. We have dedicated roadside parking bays accommodating both private vehicles, taxis, and motorbikes directly in front of and adjacent to the restaurant perimeter in Bamahu, with security observation during evening hours.

Do you cater for university events and student associations?

expand_more
Absolutely! We frequently cater for UBIDS departmental seminars, graduation gatherings, hostel get-togethers, and student executive meetings. We provide packaged takeaway boxes or tray buffets accompanied by fresh chilled juices. Bulk discounts are available with 24 hours notice.

How do takeaway orders and delivery work?

expand_more
You can either walk in and order at our express dispatch counter or phone ahead via WhatsApp. For campus delivery across UBIDS halls and Bamahu hostel clusters, our dispatch riders ensure your meals arrive piping hot within 20–35 minutes.

What payment methods do you accept at the counter?

expand_more
We accept Cash (Ghanaian Cedis) as well as Mobile Money payments (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money) at all registers for seamless speed during peak lunchtime hours.
